## Random generation

The user approved higher-level generation by the Fifth Edition tracker method on 9 October 2026. **Generate character** opens a preview with a selected Species, an optional registered origin profile, a Species/origin-filtered printed Career and a target level 1–4. The ordinary Career browser and manual entry remain unrestricted. Custom Species/Career labels have no invented starting profile: enter their choices manually. Changing the generator's origin refreshes its eligible Career list; changing its controls invalidates any old preview. Applying replaces the draft, retaining its name, chosen Species/Career and free-text background. Undo restores the previous draft. No replacement happens when merely previewing or closing the dialog.

`src/engine/marijan/generation.mjs` reuses `creationSpecies`, origin/Career availability, Specialisation options, native languages, random Talent tables, Skill/Talent eligibility, Trapping resolution, shared spell grants and the existing XP quote/purchase model. Actual dice use the shared rejection-sampled crypto helper. Selection rolls record their die size, faces, result label, timestamp and printed source; random allocation is explicitly an **app policy**, not a printed random-generation rule. Duplicate or incompatible random Species Talents are rerolled and documented. No Fourth Edition Fate/Resilience, six-at-+5/+3 Skill allocations, escalating Talent prices or eight-Skill completion thresholds are imported.

Starting generation rolls ten ordered 2d10 Characteristics with Species modifiers and randomly distributes the core's six accepted-first-roll points among level-one Career Characteristics (p. 38). It selects five distinct Species Skills at +5, native languages at +30, eight Career Skill Advances (+40 total) with the combined creation cap of +15, the Species Talent slots/random Talents and one valid Career Talent. Free spell/prayer grants use the existing acquisition-time counts and cult definitions. Basic Clothing/Dagger/Pouch, Class equipment and first-level Career Trappings follow core p. 39; rolled quantities, equipment aliases and unresolved weights/quantities reuse the inventory resolver. Starting wealth uses first-level Status/Standing (p. 39). Species and Career are selected, so there are no random Species XP or random Career Trapping/tracker rewards. Names, ambitions and optional ancestry/astrology/Longbeard choices are not invented or automatically completed.

Higher targets purchase valid in-Career +5 Characteristic/Skill Advances or Talents until the current tracker segment is full: 10 boxes, then 12, then 14. Choose equally among **available purchase groups**, then equally among valid purchases within the chosen group. Shared quotations enforce current-level eligibility, printed XP bands, Talent purchase limits and prerequisites. Each promotion costs 100 XP (p. 196); generation stops immediately upon entering the requested level. There is no arbitrary extra allotment at that level, automatic higher-level equipment, simulated downtime, or mandatory Fourth Edition Characteristic/Skill threshold. XP spent is the sum of the actual generation purchases; unspent is zero, editable afterwards.

The generated draft retains an **original generation record** with allocation policy, purchase prices and source-based reminders. This record does not change when freehand values are subsequently edited, and is included in JSON/PDF. Guided creator issues remain visible reminders in the preview/record, never Marijan export gates. Generated Skill totals/resources return to Auto, with existing permanent bonuses counted once.

**Add Species Skills**, **Add Species Talents** and **Add starting equipment** roll and add their respective choices without clearing other entries. Duplicate names use the higher existing/granted value rather than stacking grants on repeat clicks. Species Skills includes native languages; Species Talents includes their free spell/prayer grants. Starting equipment includes first-level Career equipment as well as the Class/basic items. These buttons do not roll wealth or spend XP. Their random records and changes are undoable. Separate additions may deliberately produce an unrestricted draft and do not claim a legal allocation history.

## Saves, copying and export

Schema 1 has type `wfrp-marijan`; `src/engine/marijan/model.mjs` validates its structural shape independently of creator legality. Selected entries contain reference snapshots and character-local values. Local autosave uses `wfrp-marijan-v1`; named saves use `wfrp-marijan-v1-saves`. Isolated `?verify=1` storage appends `-verification`, including for copy-source selection. Local storage failures advise/use JSON backup instead. Save character updates its named local entry and downloads a JSON backup. Load can import JSON or choose a local named save. Wrong-tool save files are rejected. Undo/Redo covers edits, additions, removals, defaults, loads, copying and New character; text/number input edits are grouped until focus leaves rather than redrawn on each keystroke. All three modes reuse the same 60-entry session history and controls; navigation and no-ops preserve redo, a new edit clears it, and reloading starts with empty history. Dice records within a draft survive Undo/Redo; replacement drafts keep independent audits.

Copy player character accepts this device's player draft or a current player JSON. It calculates the existing player snapshot through `catalogForCharacter` and `characterResult`, then creates a **new** Marijan draft without writing to the original. Existing Characteristic bonuses are included with automatic Characteristic/Movement Talent effects disabled, Skill totals and derived fields retained as manual snapshots, and copied equipment kept at its resolved unit values. The original creation/XP history is not manufactured as unrestricted purchase records. There is no conversion back to legal guided creation.

PDF uses the supplied editable character-sheet template and local pdf-lib. All entered values, ranks, overrides, sources, custom descriptions, extra entries, notes and dice logs appear in a paginated appended Marijan record. Sheet space limits abbreviate or redirect to that complete record instead of silently dropping content. The record explicitly identifies unrestricted/manual creation and does not claim verified XP history. The exact entered tracker and Corruption values remain in the record even if the supplied sheet has fewer boxes. The JSON backup preserves all character data.
