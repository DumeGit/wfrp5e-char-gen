## Implemented

- **Dark Elf** GM foundation, p. 6: printed scores, 13 Wounds, unnamed Weapon +7, optional protection and Ranged +9 at 60 yards. It does not introduce a playable Species. Core Hatred/Night Vision replace differing p. 18 summaries with specific Legacy explanations.
- **Eight templates**, pp. 6–7: Reaver Captain, Chief Mate, Corsair, Oarsman, Lookout, Beastmaster, Druchii Sorceress and the generic Warhound recipe (p. 13). Their printed applicability includes other seaborne raiders, so they use the existing unrestricted template foundation setting. Two Melee specialisations must differ. Language and Ranged use explicit core specialisations; Skills use the agreed higher-bonus interpretation.
- Sparse table bonuses are assigned by column coordinates: Corsair has +5 **Dexterity**, with no Intelligence increase; Oarsman has +5 **Fellowship**, with no Willpower increase; Lookout has +5 **Dexterity**, with no Intelligence increase. These are accurate transcription, not Legacy adaptations.
- Chief Mate's nine Talents are individually optional and skipped by default, with reversible selects. Mandatory template Talents retain their existing automatic grants. Actual differing Talent effects receive individual Legacy explanations; Strong Back and Read/Write remain untagged. Public Speaking uses the previously approved Public Speaker replacement. Generic Leather/Mail uses the approved core Light/Medium quick-armour choices. The two Hand Weapons are separate grants.
- **Beastmaster**, p. 7: Crop remains unavailable because no supplied profile defines it. The user approved the core Melee (Flail) Whip with a Legacy explanation, retaining printed Skill bonuses; Net requires an explicit melee Basic or ranged Entangling choice. Ride requires a mount specialisation. No animal is granted.
- **Four Dark Magic spells**, p. 8: Arnzipal’s Black Horror, Doombolt, Shroud of Despair and Word of Pain. Named Test Difficulties use Appendix I: Difficult −1 SL / Hard −2 SL. Shroud's zero modifier is unchanged and has no automatic Legacy tag. Shared GM Spellcaster access can select Dark Magic; no PC Colour Lore or starting spell grant is invented.
- **Reaper**, p. 10: the book explicitly directs use of Sea of Claws p. 100 Ballista plus Salvo 6. The acquired weapon reuses that reviewed profile, with no newly inferred retail price or ammunition.
- Search includes four standalone mechanical references: Dark Magic additional hits, Sea Dragon Cloak, Reaper and the generic Ravenship equipment profile. It does not index NPC/creature profiles/templates or duplicate old Talent summaries. The Ravenship preserves its actual printed table; no named ship, tactics or manager is imported. Source references preserve original wording and edition warnings; converted spell entries show separate Legacy notes.

## Approved additions

All five pending questions were answered by the user on 10 October 2026.

1. **Dark Elf Ship’s Crew**, p. 7, is a separate fixed foundation with its printed Characteristics and four sailing Talents. The mislabelled “Traits” entries are Skills. The user approved core Entertain (Singing or Storytelling) for Perform, retaining +5 and using Fellowship, with a Legacy explanation. Choose one performance and one Play instrument; these choices survive templates, save/load and history. No unprinted attacks or Dark Elf Traits are added. Bucket, Knife and Rope remain the printed named Trappings.
2. **Druchii Sorceress**, p. 7, uses an explicit Colour Lore for Arcane Magic and a matching Channelling Wind. The user-approved core Spellcaster adaptation permits its exact seven-spell mixture of Colour Lores, Witchcraft and Dark Magic, plus five Petty spells. Missing choices/counts block export with routed issues. Aethyric Attunement is ungrouped; actual changes to its effect, Attractive, Instinctive Diction and Sixth Sense have individual Legacy notes. Lore (Dark Magic) is added to the shared Skill specialisations.
3. **Warhound**, p. 13, is a Dog-only template using the core Dog foundation (p. 325), +10 Strength/Toughness and −5 Agility for Small → Average Size. Core Trained (Broken, Guard, War) requires a recorded 2d10 Fellowship roll, grants Territorial and +10 WS, and is applied once. Tracker is retained once. Broken permits ignoring Bestial; War permits ignoring Skittish for loud noises, so these Traits remain with their situational definitions. The generic recipe has a Legacy explanation; no named pack or owner is included.
4. **Sea Dragon Cloak**, p. 6, is acquired additional armour: Body 2 AP / Partial, stacking with normal or quick armour. It is an explicit optional slot for Dark Elf Reaver Captain, Chief Mate and Corsair foundations, and is also shared in the GM equipment catalogue. Price, Encumbrance and Availability remain unknown; there is no retail shop price. Its printed maintenance rule and “Body +2 AP; Partial” label are visible in the app. Under the later shared print design, full sheets and table cards list its name in Trappings and include its protection in the calculated Body Soak total, without an extra armour-description row. Additional armour is not silently classified as metal or leather for penalties/exemptions. This unchanged profile has no Legacy tag.
5. **Beastmaster equipment** follows the explicit Whip/Net choices described above. The compact selector distinguishes the two identically named core Nets by kind/group; canonical names are unchanged.

No named Flariel, Rakaria, Corvian or Cynwra profile, named warhound pack, ship manager, adventure scene, live casting or training manager is imported. Coverage and source review mark the accepted choices as implemented/adapted, Crop as unavailable and campaign material as deferred.
