## Limits and release maintenance

All questions above have now received explicit user decisions. Earlier requests for explanation were not approvals; the accepted choices came in subsequent replies. Do not infer any additional conversions from them.

For comparison, **Striding Gait (Terrain)** (core p. 127) is a different Talent: ignores movement penalties in the chosen terrain and grants +2 SL on Pursuit Tests there, without increasing Movement. It must not be used as a replacement for the hounds' Trait.

Malformed or incomplete printed entries remain visible with source warnings. Father Pedragar's Cause is not inferred from his religion; remove the unspecified printed grant and add a named Cause to resolve it. Gunna's printed Intimidation and unclear language names stay sourced references. Original animal Wounds stay printed until explicit recalculation/resizing uses core Size rules. Printed old-core page references identify Fourth Edition rather than pointing to unrelated Fifth Edition pages. Contacts, criminal organisations, burglary/fraud procedures, patrols, bounties, pathfinding/camping, live Ranald's Gamble and ongoing training are campaign systems rather than new character creation allocations.
