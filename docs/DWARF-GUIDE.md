# Dwarf Player’s Guide integration

Source: the user-supplied `Dwarf Player's Guide.pdf`, 144 pages. Printed pages match PDF positions. MarkItDown provides searchable text; PDF tables, columns and Career symbols were checked separately. The pack records its source SHA-256 and includes only structured creator content, not the source PDF.

Enable **Dwarf Player’s Guide** in Choose books. Fifth Edition core allocations, XP prices, native languages, Creature Traits and existing Talents govern unless a reviewed user decision below says otherwise. This is a creator integration; descriptions of situational or campaign effects do not automate those effects.

## Creation and background

- Eleven origins (pp. 48–50) retain printed Skills and five Talent slots, with five chosen Species Skills at +5 and native Khazalid/Reikspiel. Physical attributes remain core Dwarf values. No additional free Advances are granted.
- Each origin automatically selects its printed d100 Career table (pp. 51–52). An explicit table selection overrides this default. Regional table availability follows its actual results, including regional access absent from the core Dwarf table. Enabling the book alone does not replace core Species probabilities.
- Names use the weighted d1000 ranges on p. 39. The forename column and parent-name column are independently selectable; surnames append `sson` or `sdottir` (p. 38). Manual entry and printed-list choices remain available. Birthplace rolls on p. 41 are background; the player selects the mechanical origin separately.
- Approved table corrections: Morgrim/Nanda 738–745 instead of the overlapping 738–753; Nuln 11–12 instead of 11–13; Karak Kadrin 19–25 instead of 19–24.
- The appearance section on p. 42 refers to a table absent from the supplied PDF. Core appearance suggestions remain available; the regional ±5 modifiers are not applied to a different table. The warning is visible in Origins, Review and export.
- Optional Longbeard (p. 50) requires a chosen age of at least 120. The user approved −1 starting Fate and Fortune, retaining normal random-creation bonuses. Its social Status benefit and printed +10 on avoiding/removing Conditions remain references; they do not alter Earnings or permanent Characteristics. The ambiguous 0-Fate Resilience/Resolve fallback is deferred. This is explicitly labelled an adaptation.

## Careers and optional swaps

Ten Careers (pp. 62–78): Brewer, Doom Priest, Forge Priest, Hearth Priest, Hammerer, Ironbreaker, Karak Ranger, Runescribe, Runesmith and Thane. The printed Characteristic schemes are retained. Doom Priest is a Warrior, not an Academic.

Guide Karak Ranger and Archives I Karak Ranger remain separate profiles. When both books are enabled, the Career selector offers an explicit profile choice; Archives I remains the random-result default until the player selects the Guide profile. Profile source labels remain distinct.

Optional level replacements (pp. 54–61) are selected in Career: Guild Engineer, Outcast Engineer, Sky Pilot, Stoneshaper, Reckoner, Grudgemaster, Karak Miner, Lodefinder, Runebearer, Brother of Grimnir, Doomseeker, War-mourner, Quarreller and Thunderer. Their Skills, Talents and Trappings replace that level. Only explicitly printed replacement Characteristics change the core scheme. Changes are locked after XP spending and clear dependent Career choices. Axefighter is shown unavailable: its Strength replacement duplicates Fifth Edition Soldier’s level-one Strength, and the user chose to defer it. Outcast Engineer’s unspecialised Entertain entry remains unavailable rather than inventing a specialisation. Conditional Up in Arms targets do not invent Species availability for their parent Career.

The optional p. 55 equipment/weapon Skill swaps have a checkbox in Career and actual selections in Gear/Skills:

- Career Hand Weapon → Dwarf Axe or Dwarf Warhammer.
- Career Cavalry/Fencing/Parry weapon → Shield or Pistol with ammunition.
- Career Bow weapon and its ammunition → Dwarf Crossbow or Dwarf Handgun with ammunition.
- Career Riding Horse/Warhorse → Full Plate Armour and Helm.
- Corresponding Career weapon Skill choices gain the printed matching alternatives, preserving original options. Species Skills, Class equipment and Ride are not replaced.

Replacing a bow removes its old associated Career arrows. Unspecified ammunition quantities and Full Plate Armour composition remain unresolved; the app does not invent ammunition packs or armour pieces. Changing the optional setting before XP spending clears Career allocations/gear while keeping Species selections.

Approved naming mappings: Huffer → Pilot; Advisor → Adviser; Seaman → Sailor; Fearless (All) → selectable core enemy group; Protective Runes → Protection Runes. Previously approved core equivalents also apply: Strider → Striding Gait, Tunnel Rat → Tunnel Fighter, Warleader → War Leader, Set Traps → Set Trap, and Ranged (Thrown) → Ranged (Throwing). Printed `Gromil` is normalised to Gromril. The user-approved Guide `Dwarf Hammer` → Dwarf Warhammer naming mismatch uses the Guide table profile; this spelling does not remap Archives I’s differently named weapons.

## Skills, Talents and runes

Runesmithing uses Dexterity; Lore (Runes) uses Intelligence; Sail (Skycraft) uses Agility (p. 80). These Dwarf-specific profiles are distinct from ordinary Lore/Sail. Ordinary Sail does not unlock Skycraft. Exceptions remain GM decisions; there is no acknowledgement checkbox. Printed specialisations extend existing core groups without extra free points.

Twenty Talent records on pp. 80–83 retain user-approved printed limits and effects. Talents with omitted Fourth Edition per-rank Test SL bonuses or converted Difficulties carry Legacy/adaptation warnings; compatible unchanged profiles do not. Repeated purchases use Fifth Edition’s 100 XP price. Dynamic limits count all free and paid purchases of the same base Talent, across specialised rune names. Rune Magic’s cap is Intelligence Bonus + Willpower Bonus; Master Rune Magic’s cap is Willpower Bonus. Crew Commander remains unavailable under the earlier Up in Arms decision, even when both books are enabled.

Each Ancestral Grudge purchase needs a distinct culture/faction. Printed campaign XP rewards are descriptions, never added to creator XP. Undo removes excess choice slots.

The 71 rune entries (pp. 127–132) include ordinary/master Weapon, Armour, Talisman, Protection and Engineering runes and three Doom Runes. Rune Talent choices restrict forms and master/ordinary status. Already learned runes cannot be bought again. Learning any Master Rune grants knowledge of the three Doom Runes once, without extra XP or tracker boxes. Learning never grants enchanted equipment or an Anvil of Doom. Rune descriptions, source pages and knowledge appear in the folio, Review and editable PDF/companion record.

Named old Test Difficulties are converted to core SL modifiers; numeric situational bonuses remain printed descriptions. Advantage references use Momentum. Inconsistent names within printed rune effect prose are retained as source wording, not silently invented errata (Force/Impact, Clear Sight/Clear Seeing, Truth/Disclosure and Far Sight/Farseeing). Rune forging, activation, Anvil operation and campaign progression are deferred.

## Gear and precedence

The Guide adds 19 portable weapon profiles (pp. 93–94), seven armour profiles (p. 95), ammunition and selected portable Trappings (pp. 93–97). Handgun/Pistol damage already includes their printed ammunition modifiers; these are not applied twice. New situational qualities remain reference rules. Printed armour penalties stay visible as references alongside core equipment calculations.

Gromril, Shield Platform, Oathstone and Anvil of Doom have profiles/reference weights and printed material-value descriptions, but are excluded from ordinary shop purchases by user decision. Unspecified Gromril Suit contents and ammunition Encumbrance remain unknown. Learning runes does not supply any of these items. Slayers cannot purchase armour or shields (p. 59), with an explanation next to blocked items.

Page 92 explicitly supersedes Archives I’s Dwarf weapons. With both books enabled, all eight older weapon profiles and shop rows are withdrawn: Bearded Axe, Dwarf Hammer, Slayer’s Axe, Dwarf Handgun, Dwarf Pistol, Dwarf Crossbow, Drakefire Pistol and Cinderblast Bomb. The Guide’s profiles remain. No one-to-one replacement is invented for the three different names. Archives I alone remains unchanged, as do its Elf/Halfling weapons and earlier ammunition-price decisions. Precedence is declarative, source-labelled and independent of book selection order.

Vehicles, artillery, crews, engineering installations, forging, play-time grudges, Criminal earnings exceptions, live combat effects and deity campaign obligations remain outside the creator. Descriptive Trappings without profiles remain named and unresolved.

## Maintenance and verification

`scripts/extract-dwarf-guide.py` stages extraction outside the published app. `scripts/prepare-dwarf-guide.py --output-dir ../tmp/pdfs/dwarf-guide-prepared` applies reviewed conversions. Copy only `manifest.json` and files declared by its `files` object into the pack; never copy full page text or raw extraction into `dist`.

Tests cover all 128 combinations of earlier supplements in both load orders, regional tables, all new Career schemes and complete allocations, name boundaries, Longbeard values, unavailable choices, optional swaps, shared rune limits, Doom grants, grudges, Species restrictions, shop precedence, schema failures and all 556 editable PDF fields. Browser verification uses an isolated draft; exported PDFs are rendered and checked independently of field values.
