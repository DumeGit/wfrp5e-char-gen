Salzenmund already supplies its seven generic foundations and four Spite templates. Archives III keeps the earlier deferral of six familiar profiles, and its remaining stat blocks belong to named characters. The review retains reusable unnamed encounter groups and distinctive monster stat blocks, including the three Altdorf monsters, and excludes named campaign NPCs and named companions. Mechanically distinct same-name profiles retain their individual source pages. Exact reprints identified during review use one foundation, including the draught dog reused in Ubersreik Adventures III and the Flying Death Skull reprinted in The Horned Rat Companion.

The profiles preserve the printed baseline and use the core default Average Size where the source supplies no Size. Core Small Wounds and core primary melee Size Damage are applied where appropriate. A dash remains an absent value. Tiny Wounds and printed dice expressions may need manual entry. Existing definitions supply compatible Skills, Talents and Traits. Specific changes such as Stride to Sprinter carry Legacy notes. Unresolved hit-location coverage, equipment alternatives, conditional effects and source-only attack rules are documented for manual handling. Required unresolved Traits and missing parameters use the workshop's existing export checks.

The three River Mutant options are separate fixed profiles because each changes attacks, armour or equipment. Animated Sign and the metal and stone statue templates use the depicted creature's foundation. Adjust Size when the depiction and creature differ. Metal adds 3 Armour and stone adds 4. Bog Zombie applies to the core Zombie. The Knight of the Fiery Heart grants its three listed Sigmar Miracles. The selectable Unmarred Flesh Cultist variant represents the source's post-encounter condition.

The Harpy foundation preserves the distinct characteristic row on p. 158. The same page repeats the Minotaur's traits, Skill and equipment. The workshop flags the missing Harpy rules before export. The Sigmarite Flagellant, Knight of the White Wolf and Minor Noble are fixed foundations that carry their printed attack and equipment differences. Chaos Spawn grants four printed Traits to the core Gor or Mutant and leaves the 2d10 mutation count and the specified Tzeentch table results to the GM. Its temporary Gor transformation belongs to the spell. The Purple Hand Cultist template uses the core Cultist and grants its listed Skills and Secret Identity. Career details and the mutation chance are manual. The senior cultist and magus instructions depend on separate Career development and source Talents. Shapechanging and group casting are conditional play rules. The Horned Rat Companion's variable animal builds remain optional traits or source notes, and the junior Yellow Fang template applies to the core Cultist. The senior cultist guidance leaves Skill totals and the NPC's background to the GM. Named Ubersreik chief and acrobat examples remain excluded. Per-book coverage files record deferred packages and pages.
