# Archives of the Empire: Volume I

Installed as the optional `archives-i` pack, version 1.0.0. Enable it in **Choose books**. It can run with the core alone or alongside Up in Arms. Changing enabled books starts a new WIP character. No source PDF is bundled or published.

Source: the user's supplied `Archives of the Empire - Vol I.pdf`, 96 pages, SHA-256 `d07f2cbd9faf64f3e979ee249c44bfbfa3e8163b47a2fc678e6749700920c5d7`. Printed page numbers match PDF page positions. MarkItDown provided the full text; Career blocks, coloured Characteristic symbols, clan table and equipment tables were checked against the original PDF.

## Included creator material

- Four Careers: Ghost Strider (p. 88), Fieldwarden (p. 89), Karak Ranger (p. 90), Badger Rider (p. 91). Their printed eight first-level Skill options, four levels, Characteristics, Status and Trappings are retained. Fifth Edition free allocations, XP and tracker rules apply.
- Twelve optional Reikland Halfling clan profiles (p. 32): Ashfield, Brambledown, Brandysnap, Hayfoot, Hollyfoot, Hayfoot-Hollyfoot, Lostpockets, Lowhaven, Rumster, Skelfsider, Thorncobble and Tumbleberry.
- A Mootland origin enforcing Fieldwarden/Badger Rider access (p. 87), and the conditional Thorncobble Noble option (p. 31).
- Three Eonir kindreds (p. 78): Forestborn/Faniour, Cityborn/Toriour and Younger/Harioth, including the additional Youngblood Talent for Younger.
- Fourteen weapon profiles and four ammunition shop entries (pp. 92–94). All existing core profiles, prices and weights remain unchanged. Equipment access is not restricted by Species; the book says the printed Availability assumes a strong local presence of the associated Species and that some weapons are not sold to outsiders. This qualification is displayed in the shop and exported record.
- Printed additional specialisations, including Bowyer, Farmer, Moot, Necromancy, Badgers, Badger and Dwarf Holds and Routes. They feed the core grouped Skill and derived Craftsman/Savant selectors.

## Explicit user conversion decisions — October 3, 2026

### Halfling clans

Keep Fifth Edition physical attributes, native Haffennaff/Reikspiel and five distinct Species Skills at +5. Use the seven common Skills plus the four printed clan Skills. Map the older Language (Mootish) name to Haffennaff and Resistance (Chaos) to Resistant (Chaos). Omit the older Small Talent because Fifth Edition handles Halfling size separately. Keep Acute Sense (Taste), Night Vision and Resistant (Chaos); one clan Talent replaces one of the core's two random Talents. There are still five Species Talents, plus the usual free Career Talent. Grouped choices such as Craftsman (Any) expand into actual core/supplement specialisations rather than granting a placeholder.

### Eonir

All three kindreds use Wood Elf starting attributes, Skills, Talents, native languages, appearance suggestions and physical details. Forestborn and Younger use Wood Elf Career availability and its core roll table. Cityborn uses High Elf Career availability and its core roll table, without changing Species roll probabilities or copying High Elf Species benefits. Cityborn is explicitly described as a GM option.

The sheet identifies every Eonir as **Wood Elf (Eonir)**. Cityborn's Class field contains the Class and **(Cityborn)** on two readable lines; the companion record also names Toriour and the complete origin. Younger receives Youngblood as an additional zero-XP creation grant, maximum 1. Its contextual lower-Status rule applies when comparing with other Eonir who lack Youngblood; ordinary Career Status is not reduced globally. The description and record explain this. No older per-rank Talent Test bonus is imported. Losing Youngblood after a campaign trial is outside creator scope.

### Conditional Careers

Fieldwarden and Badger Rider require the explicit Mootland origin. It retains core Halfling creation choices; no Reikland clan profile is assumed. Badger Rider displays the printed requirement to convince the GM and attempt the badger war cry. The app explains this table requirement; it cannot verify a conversation or performance at the gaming table.

Thorncobble Noble requires Noble Blood before creation/export is complete. It may be the free first-level Career Talent, a random starting Talent, or an actual eligible XP purchase. Undoing its purchase restores the validation issue. Fifth Edition core p. 76 already permits Halfling Nobles, so enabling Archives I does **not** remove Noble from other core Halfling origins; the approved requirement applies to the selected Thorncobble profile.

Printed optional substitutions (p. 87) are offered after a random result: Wood Elf Bounty Hunter → Ghost Strider; Dwarf Messenger → Karak Ranger; Mootland Halfling Roadwarden → Fieldwarden; Mootland Halfling Soldier → Badger Rider. They are choices, not extra random rolls. Original core probabilities and first/three-roll bonus eligibility remain. The older printed “Road Warden” name refers to the core's “Roadwarden”. Up in Arms' optional military roll remains a separate choice; selecting a Career never bypasses its current Species/origin restrictions.

### Career Talent mismatches

Use current core definitions and established older-name mappings: Strider → Striding Gait, Trick Riding → Trick Rider, Tunnel Rat → Tunnel Fighter. Fleet Footed and Scale sheer Surface are spelling/capitalisation variants of Fleet-footed and Scale Sheer Surface. The mappings are recorded on the Career data.

Fieldwarden's unspecialised Fearless offers core enemy-group choices. Savant (Moot terrain) uses Savant (Moot). Karak Ranger's printed Savant (Dwarf Holds and Routes) is retained as a Lore specialisation; the core requirement to possess an Advance in that Lore still applies. River Guide OR Gunner retains both printed alternatives; it does not grant either automatically.

Ghost Strider's Windwraith **Lip Reading is printed in the Skill list**, but Fifth Edition defines Lip Reading as a Talent (core p. 122). At the user's request the entry remains visibly unavailable in the Career path, with an explanation. It is excluded from Skill purchases/advances and does not become an extra Talent option. The mismatch is preserved in the exported source/creation record.

### Equipment and overlapping prices

New named profiles retain printed statistics and inherent properties. Blackpowder and Engineering weapons include the p. 93 footnote's Blackpowder and Damaging Qualities. Drakefire Pistol's critical-hit Ablaze effect, Blackbriar Javelin's earth-rest/Poisoned/resistance rule, Starfire's critical-hit Ablaze effect and Swiftshiver's additional-arrow requirement are described. Drakefire Shot's unsafe use in other Blackpowder weapons (p. 94) is also described. These are contextual references, not combat automation or permanent bonuses. Blackbriar's Challenging (+0) Test Difficulty becomes +0 SL under core Appendix I p. 364.

Precision Shot and Powder has the same modifiers but different prices: Archives I p. 93 gives **3 pennies**, Up in Arms p. 102 gives **3 shillings**. User decision: use Archives I's price when that book is enabled alone, and Up in Arms' price when both are enabled. The explicit `supersededBy` declaration prevents duplicate shop entries, is independent of selection order, retains the active item's source, and records the decision in the companion PDF. There is no silent general “last book wins” policy.

Printed Entangling OR Throwing and Basic OR Cavalry weapon choices use the corresponding actual profiles. Counted lowercase arrows and differently capitalised Trade tools resolve as spelling variants without changing their quantities or weights. Descriptive names with no unambiguous profile retain their original text and unknown weight: personally crafted Elf Bow, Selection of Traps, Map of local area, Sling stones, Blackpowder Weapon with uncounted Ammunition, Grappling Hook & Rope, Boiled Leather Breastplate, Tamed Badger with Saddle/Tack and other unspecified Trappings. No badger statistics, weight, purchase price, carrying capacity or saddle contents are invented. Existing unapproved Up in Arms Trapping aliases remain unresolved.

## Deferred material

Provincial/settlement descriptions, clan society, NPC statistics, adventure hooks, campaign kindred trials, animal combat/barding management, follower management and later Career changes are outside this character creator. Character/GM notes can record fictional details; no mechanical province benefits or new random background tables are invented from narrative descriptions. There are no additional Spells or Miracles in this book's creator material.

## Extraction and verification

`scripts/extract-archives-i.py` stages only creator Career/equipment data outside the published app in `../tmp/pdfs/archives-i-review`. It reads the user-supplied PDF, preserves columns and coloured schemes, and records its hash. `scripts/build-archives-i.py` applies the reviewed mappings and approved profiles; the clan table and Eonir passage are transcribed into the builder with their source pages and decisions. Run the extraction with Python containing pdfplumber/pypdf, then the builder. Do not publish the source or staged full-book text.

Automated coverage checks opt-in isolation, standalone/combined books and price precedence, clan allocations and grouped Talents, all four Career schemes and legal creation, Eonir attributes/availability/tables, Youngblood's grant/limit, Thorncobble purchase/undo requirements, optional substitutions, weapon choices/ammunition and malformed references. PDF checks preserve all 556 editable fields and verify the Eonir Species/Class values. Browser checks use the isolated `?verify=1` draft, with desktop and narrow mobile layouts.
