## Included creation content

Enable `high-elf` in Choose books. It depends on core. Eleven High Elf origins include ten Ulthuan kingdoms and Sea Elf (pp. 54–58). They retain core physical attributes, native languages, Fate/Fortune and five distinct Species Skills at +5, with **all printed Talent slots**, including six where printed. Sea Elf enclave and kingdom of heritage are background, not a second regional allocation. Uncouth Uranai affects Standing only in the printed social context.

Five regional d100 Career tables (p. 59) cover every result and become the origin’s default. An explicit core-table choice overrides that default. User-approved corrections: Outer Kingdoms Guard **89–90**, keeping Knight 91–92; Avelorn Scholar **13–17**, keeping Artisan 18–21. Names map Advisor → Adviser, Huffer → Pilot, Seaman → Sailor and Bawd → Knave, using core definitions.

Six new Careers: Sea Guard, Swordmaster, Shadow Warrior, Merchant Adventurer, Aestheticist and Mage. Mage includes levels 1–4. Ordinary Fifth Edition free Advances, prices, tracker boxes and promotion apply. Animal Care and Sail specialisations are context notes with one core total; Animal Training remains grouped. No obsolete fourth-edition Skill totals are invented.

The pp. 60–63 Ulthuan modifications are per-character effective profiles, not replacements of core catalog records. They feed Skills, Talents, Trappings, Status, shop/inventory context, review and export. Soldier/Artillerist exclude Blackpowder Career choices; Cavalryman replaces its Blackpowder option with Track and the starting firearm with Elf Bow/ammunition. Artisan uses Nobles, Lore (Magic), patronage and the Exalted Maker title; Merchant/Wizard licence changes, Protagonist’s Sword and regional Standing changes are included. Printed Archer, Artillerist, Light Cavalry and Camp Follower access requires both Guides’ context and Up in Arms.

Pit Fighter’s Feint and Elven ship Sailor are optional Career-section choices. **User-approved Sailor adaptation:** core Athletics remains at level 1 once; remove Consume Alcohol, replace Melee (Brawling) with Melee (Basic), and add Intuition at level 2. It does not grant extra free Advances. The optional Elven ship Sailor variant also applies to Sea Elf origins; the automatic Ulthuan changes do not.

## Elder creation

Optional birth era, exact printed age dice and past Careers are in Origins; allocations are in Starting Skills (pp. 52–53). Each prior era excluding Time of Ending grants **10 chosen or 15 rolled individual points**, with at most five in any resolved Skill per era. Rolled Careers retain their exact table/face; keeping the recorded result grants 15, choosing another grants 10. No extra roll can be used to fish for another 15-point Career in the same era.

These points add to ordinary Fifth Edition free Skills and are exempt from its normal free-creation cap. They do not grant Talents, equipment or tracker boxes. Their actual points feed totals and XP bands, including the existing rule requiring a partial band to be completed with +1 purchases before +5. Imported roll histories remain editable records, not proof of randomness.

Approved conversion: the old lost extra Fate/Resilience point becomes a choice of **−1 starting Fate or −1 starting Fortune**. Time of Shadows sets both to zero, including ordinary creation/Luck bonuses; Blood of Aenarion takes priority. Other printed burdens accumulate, including five starting Corruption from Time of Incursion and Prejudice (Humans) from Time of the Sage. Only the oldest era’s weekly Yenlui Test applies; ongoing Tests/Endeavours are references.

Several printed age formulas cross their printed era bands. Preserve the exact formula and result, with a note; do not clamp or silently reassign the era. Manual ages must fit the selected band. Updating age replaces the generated appearance age. Exports include each past Career, points, burdens and checked Corruption boxes.

## Blood of Aenarion and spirituality

Optional ancestry (p. 51): a real d100 roll has 5% for a Noble Career or possession of Noble Blood, 1% otherwise; the chance at the roll is recorded. It can also be chosen with the GM. It grants one Fate and Magical or Martial Prodigy. Its rolled Psychology uses core p. 189 mental-corruption effects, including permanent Characteristic changes, without treating it as a Mutation or granting an extra mutation count.

Magical Prodigy halves spell learning XP. **Ritual memorisation is included by explicit user interpretation**, labelled in the quote/ledger and export. It does not discount Sword-dancing techniques. Martial Prodigy halves the seven printed Talent prices only when that purchase is otherwise legal; it grants no new Career access. Undo recalculates XP and learned options.

Yenlui is optional background with age-dependent casting/Test reference modifiers (p. 47), not permanent score changes or live state tracking. The two printed d10 tables of Dreams/Obsessions offer choice, real roll or custom text (pp. 48–50), with interpretations and conditional benefits recorded. They grant no unconditional XP or passive bonuses.

## Magic and techniques

59 new spells: six Petty, eight Elven Arcane, 45 High Magic including the learnable Vaul/Mathlann/Hoeth lists. No core duplicates are replaced. Descriptions, CN, range, target, duration, prerequisites and printed pages are preserved. Named old Test Difficulties use Appendix I SL conversions; other numeric bonuses and situational effects remain descriptions. The esoteric faith lists require a tutor/pertinent reading materials (p. 99), recorded as a reference rather than an invented acknowledgement gate.

Mage specifically requires **10 individual points in the previous Colour’s Channelling and four qualifying spells** before another Colour Lore, up to WP Bonus (p. 79). Other Elf Careers retain core’s eight-spell requirement. Relearning the same generic Arcane name through another Lore increases its price count but does not count as a different qualifying training spell. Mage offers all eight Colour Channelling specialisations for paid Career advancement, without extra free points.

Elven Arcane requires both printed Colour Talents, is learned only once and belongs to the latest acquired Colour Lore for learning prices/training counts. Student Mage promotion requires four of this guide’s six Petty spells. Blessed by Isha requires completed Student Mage, WP 80, Petty Magic/four guide spells, and all eight Lores with 10 Channelling points/four qualifying spells each (pp. 79–80).

Channelling (Qhaysh) cannot exceed any of the eight Colour Channelling totals. High Magic requires it and Blessed by Isha. **High Magic’s one-purchase limit is an approved adaptation**: the book describes ranks without printing a maximum. No free spell is invented. New spells cost 200 XP per inclusive Intelligence Bonus band of **currently known** High Magic spells: up to IB costs 200, up to 2×IB costs 400, and so on (p. 83). Rituals and Sword-dancing do not enter these counts.

Martial Arts retains its printed WS Bonus maximum; Lileath’s Blessing retains Initiative Bonus. Situational rank effects are references. Sword-dancing grants Ritual of Cleansing free; the remaining nine techniques cost 100 XP per technique currently known (pp. 68–69). They earn no tracker boxes and appear in Magic, folio, ledger and export. Required weapons, SL targets and combat effects are references, not combat automation.

## Equipment and scope

23 new gear/reference records, one weapon and 11 armour profiles (pp. 32–37). Core prices/profiles stay intact. New prices are qualified as High Elf market/outpost prices. Unique Greatsword of Hoeth, Ithilmar/Dragon armour and Ithiltaen values are reference values rather than ordinary shop offers. **Ithiltaen AP is blank in the source and remains unresolved.** Combs have no invented prices/weights. Unspecified mounts, ships, magical weapons and descriptive Career Trappings retain unresolved statistics.

Ithilmar/Dragon profiles use core plate layering/penalties; White Lion hide uses the leather layer. Inherent printed Qualities remain. Extra printed numeric penalties and conditional Dragon heat/fire protection remain descriptions; no general AP doubling or universal Magic Resistance is invented. Armour/containers are worn, weapons equipped and belongings packed automatically. New herbs, wine and six enchanted items retain their printed prices/effects as qualified references; bought raw staffs do not become crafted enchanted weapons.

Deferred by user choice: Archmage fifth Career level and three advanced priest Career branches. All their learnable spell profiles are available through the supported High Magic handler. Campaign intrigue, career changes, ship/crew combat, crafting, sacrifices, generated creatures, weekly psychology/Yenlui changes and Dream fulfilment remain future manager systems.
