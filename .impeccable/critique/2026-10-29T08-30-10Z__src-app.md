## Design specificity and overall impression

**Keep Imperial Desk.** Oak navigation, parchment, chapter typography, mode artwork and accents make this feel authored for tabletop record keeping. Printed baselines, source references and explicit overrides reinforce that identity through behavior. A generic dashboard replacement would lose useful character.

The largest opportunity is to separate **making a decision, consulting a reference and moving on**. These often occupy one long page with similar weight. The product's inherent rules complexity should remain; the extra traversal should not.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey

**Moderate avoidable load:** three checklist weaknesses in long creation views — chunking, minimal choices and progressive disclosure. Eight creation pages, nine Marijan section links and ten real Characteristics are not defects by themselves. The issue is combining alternatives, current selection, full reference detail and continuation into one uninterrupted task path.

The desk creates a strong opening. Inspectable totals and visible readiness give reassurance. Long scrolling after a choice is ready creates the main valley. Review is a satisfying peak; its ending should also explain reusable backup and the separate play workflow, rather than implying that PDF output ends every journey.

## Minor observations

- During book setup the compact selector says 1. Origins while the content says Choose your books. Represent the setup context accurately without turning it into a ninth creation step.
- Marijan's two-column phone Characteristics could distinguish each editable value and adjacent calculated total more clearly. Accessible names are present; no clipping or overlap was established.
- Bestiary's phone setup stack pushes its first profile result below the initial screen; tighten introductory/filter hierarchy without shrinking touch targets. A missing profile also looks like an error before any attempt; neutral required-state styling may be calmer.
- Shared reference reading, complete Career membership tables and legal content remain available and reflow in sampled phone captures. Long genuine reference material should not be removed merely to reduce page length.

## Questions to consider
