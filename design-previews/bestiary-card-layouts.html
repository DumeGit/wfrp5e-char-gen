Bestiary card layouts

Three compact alternatives. Each uses the same character, descriptions and text size. These are layout studies; the current exports are unchanged.

A · Record grid

A narrow section-label column replaces repeated heading rows. The descriptions stay together at almost full width.

B · Combat grid

Compact numbers and short lists share rows. Weapons and long Trait descriptions keep the full card width.

C · Flowing columns

Text balances between two columns. Short abilities pack tightly; long descriptions wrap more and can take extra space.

For maximum density: C, with content-sized packing for the page. C packs these examples most tightly. Choose B if you prefer long descriptions at full width; it is easier to follow while running a fight.

A further page-layout improvement would let cards have different heights and pack them down two A4 columns. That removes unused space below short creatures, but produces uneven cut lines. Fixed six-card cut lines can stay as an option.

White paper, greyscale ink, no decorative padding. Height measurements below the previews are browser estimates; final PDF fitting must still use its actual font measurements. No descriptions have been shortened or omitted for these comparisons.
